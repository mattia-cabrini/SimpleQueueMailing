SimpleQueueMailing

To execute:
	SimpleQueueMailing /path/to/config/file

To show this help screen:
	SimpleQueueMailing help

To get an examle config:
	SimpleQueueMailing config

Files ending in "noeml" are picked up from QueueIn immediately, even while
they are still being written. Write each message under a temporary name that
does not end in "noeml" on the same filesystem, then rename it: the rename is
atomic.
